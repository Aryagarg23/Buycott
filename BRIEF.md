# BRIEF — Buycott (repo: Aryagarg23/Buycott)
Slug: buycott. Hackathon: MakeUC 2023, November 2023. Prizes: 1st Place Overall, Best Social Issues Hack, Best Use of Google Cloud, Wolfram Research Award.
Team: Arya Garg (fine-tuned RoBERTa stance model, Google Cloud database), Aniruddhan Ramesh (Flask backend, Flutter charts), Ary Sharma, Kaaustaaub Shankar. Original team repo: https://github.com/KaaustaaubShankar/BuycottMakeUC2023
What: scan a product barcode → resolve parent company → NLP over GDELT news to score the company's stance on social issues so people can boycott (or buycott) with sourced evidence instead of vibes.
Tech: Flutter/Dart, Flask, Google Cloud, Google Vision API, fine-tuned RoBERTa, GDELT, HuggingFace.
Devpost: https://devpost.com/software/buycott
Prototype idea: tiny lexicon-based stance scorer over ~12 synthetic headlines for 4 fake companies, chart: diverging horizontal bars (blue one stance, burnt orange the other, neutral mid) of stance score per company with headline counts annotated.
